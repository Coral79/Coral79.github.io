About

I am a PhD candidate at the University of Tübingen, supervised by Prof. Gerard Pons-Moll and Prof. Andreas Geiger. I am part of the Real Virtual Humans group and the Autonomous Vision Group.

My research centers on compositional human motion and interaction. I explore how complex human behaviors can be represented, generated, and understood through the composition of body-part motions, actions, and interactions with objects. I want to build controllable models that can combine what they have learned to create new motions and interactions, even in situations they have never seen before.

Previously, I received my MSc in Electrical Engineering and Information Technology from ETH Zürich. I completed my master's thesis at the Max Planck Institute for Informatics, working with Marc Habermann and Christian Theobalt.

I'm always happy to chat about research, new ideas, and collaborations. Feel free to email me!
